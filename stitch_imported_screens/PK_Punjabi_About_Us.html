The PK Punjabi Chronicle

Rooted in the timeless lanes of Amritsar near the sanctified Golden Temple, PK Punjabi began as a modest clay tandoor hearth orchestrated by Lala Puran Chand Sodhi in the winter of 1968.

Taking our culinary fire to the vibrant heart of Chandigarh, we continue to preserve recipes handed down via unwritten family diaries. Every single gravy is cooked exclusively in thick, unlined copper deghs over wood and sweet coal, sealed under dough purdah for royal slow-dum perfection.
